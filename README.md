# Hi, I'm Will Chan

I'm a product manager with a habit of turning “what if?” into something you can click, play with, or occasionally break.

AI is both part of my product practice and a hobby I happily get carried away with. At work, I'm interested in how it can help us explore problems, test ideas, and build more useful products. Outside work, the questions get a little stranger: what would a book sound like? Could a photo become a tiny pixel companion? What happens if I build 30 apps in 30 days?

This GitHub is where those two sides meet. You'll find tools for product thinking alongside music experiments, games, and playful prototypes—an ongoing exploration of what AI makes possible, and what's actually worth making.

## Selected projects

### [PM Coaching App](https://github.com/willchanpm/app-02-pm-coaching-app)

A prototype for making career development more concrete: compare a product manager's self-assessment, manager feedback, and role expectations to identify areas for growth.

[Explore the app](https://30-in-30-app-02.vercel.app) · [Read the code](https://github.com/willchanpm/app-02-pm-coaching-app)

### [Page Score](https://github.com/willchanpm/Vibe-Code-Music)

An experimental soundtrack for reading. Book metadata shapes AI-generated ambient music, accompanied by a reactive 3D visualiser.

[See the project and screenshot](https://github.com/willchanpm/Vibe-Code-Music#readme) · Runs locally with your own API keys

### [Tamagotcha](https://github.com/willchanpm/code-issue-collective)

A hackathon prototype exploring a simple idea: turn a friend's photo into a little 8-bit companion. The current work focuses on generating sprites and audio; the interactive companion and QR-sharing experience are still in progress.

[Explore the prototype](https://code-issue-collective.vercel.app) · [Read the code and current status](https://github.com/willchanpm/code-issue-collective#readme)
